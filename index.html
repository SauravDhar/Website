Systems built

The paperwork nobody sees

Every event runs on documents built before anyone walks in — the reason a hundred moving parts arrive on time, once, and in the right quantity.

Document 01

Procurement Indent Tracker

Line-item indents per event, with rolling totals against budget and vendor allocation — the kitchen orders once, nothing arrives twice, and every rupee traces back to a dish.

i
Document 02

Event Operations Order

Turns a client brief into one operational document — timings, headcount, menu flow, staffing, equipment — signed off before every event so nobody improvises on the day.

ii
Document 03

Daily Operations Tracker

A shift-level checklist and log of setup, service and closing against SOP — a gap surfaces the same day, not in a debrief a week later. Built in Excel and ReportLab.
